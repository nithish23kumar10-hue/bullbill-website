Inside BullBill

One app that handles
your whole restaurant

Every part of your daily operation — from the front door to the kitchen to the cash drawer — covered in one place.

Safe Logins for Every Role

Owner, branch manager, cashier, waiter, kitchen — each person gets their own login and only the permissions they need. You stay in control, always.

Tables & Floor Management

Set up your floors and tables once. Merge, split, or transfer tables mid-service. Staff always know which table is free, occupied, or waiting for the bill.

Menu — Always Up to Date

Add dishes, change prices, or mark something sold-out for the day in seconds. Changes reflect instantly on your QR digital menu too — no reprinting anything.

Billing & Payments

UPI, cash, card, split bills between friends. GST, VAT, and Excise line items handled correctly on the same bill for bars and licensed venues.

Kitchen Display Screen

A screen in the kitchen shows every new order the moment the waiter confirms it, with live item-by-item status. No paper, no shouting, no delays.

QR Digital Menu & Self-Ordering

One QR per branch, customer-facing and self-service. Shows only what you've enabled — browse-only menu, takeaway ordering, or dine-in with a real table picker. Choose from multiple visual themes to match your restaurant's look.

Customer Loyalty & Rewards

Points on every visit, punch-card rewards, and a redeemable rewards catalog. Send a compliment or a win-back offer to lapsed customers straight from your phone.

WhatsApp Business Integration

Automated order-placed, ready-for-pickup, cancelled, and invoice messages. Run marketing campaigns with a template builder — use your own WhatsApp number or a BullBill-managed one.

Reports & Analytics

Z-reports, daily revenue trends, top-selling dishes, staff performance, and prep-time insights — in one glance, per branch or across your whole chain.

Inventory & Stock Tracking

Raw materials linked to recipes, low-stock alerts before you run out mid-service, stock verification, and a wastage log to spot losses early.

Accounts Management

GSTR-1 export in JSON and Excel, credit/debit notes, customer GSTIN capture for B2B invoicing, HSN codes flowing from menu to invoice, and a full immutable audit trail.

Built for Bars & Licensed Outlets

Mix GST food items with VAT or Excise liquor lines on the same bill, correctly split. MRP price-lock for government price-controlled items keeps you compliant automatically.
